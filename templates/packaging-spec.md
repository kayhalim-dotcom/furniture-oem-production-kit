# Packaging Specification

## Packaging requirements

- Use corrugated board with adequate compression strength
- Add corner protectors for edges and corners
- Insert foam or EPE between parts where required
- Label carton with product code, quantity, and destination

## Recommended carton layout

- Components wrapped individually
- Flat-pack arrangement for efficient handling
- Assembly hardware in sealed bag with part reference
- Product manual included in pack where required

## Dispatch information

- Product reference: LSC-01
- Carton size: 960 x 500 x 2050 mm
- Estimated weight: 54 kg
- Handling: Fragile surfaces protected
