# Production release checklist

Use one checklist per product revision/order. Attach evidence or controlled-document references; a checked box alone is not approval.

## Product definition
- [ ] Customer brief, use case, quantity, destination market, and responsible owner recorded
- [ ] Product code, part numbers, revision, units, and lifecycle status checked
- [ ] Overall assembly and part drawings approved, dimensioned, and revision controlled
- [ ] Datums, joints, feature locations/sizes/depths, tolerances, edge and finish callouts defined
- [ ] BOM, cut list, machining operations, and assembly instructions cross-checked against drawings

## Materials and suppliers
- [ ] Material grades, thicknesses, supplier, finish/decor, and approval samples documented
- [ ] Hardware make/model, finish, supplier data, and fixing details approved
- [ ] Special process instructions and traceability requirements defined
- [ ] Substitutions and customer approvals documented

## Validation and quality
- [ ] Prototype fit/function/stability review completed and recorded
- [ ] Applicable customer/market safety, emissions, chemical, durability, fire, and labeling requirements identified
- [ ] Required test plans/reports approved by responsible qualified parties
- [ ] Inspection plan has method, sampling plan where applicable, and measurable acceptance criteria
- [ ] Deviations and corrective actions closed or formally dispositioned

## Packaging and logistics
- [ ] Pack-out, protection, carton dimensions, verified weights, and labels approved
- [ ] Packaging/transit validation completed for intended distribution route
- [ ] Assembly/care instructions and spare-hardware policy confirmed where required

## Approval and distribution
- [ ] Design owner approval / reference: ____________________ Date: __________
- [ ] Engineering approval / reference: ___________________ Date: __________
- [ ] Quality approval / reference: _______________________ Date: __________
- [ ] Customer/compliance approval as applicable: __________ Date: __________
- [ ] Released package archived; supplier recipients and effective date recorded
- [ ] Obsolete copies withdrawn and change history updated

**Release decision:** ☐ Approved for production ☐ Not approved ☐ Conditional (attach restrictions)
